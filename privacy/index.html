Privacy, in plain language.

Effective September 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Read Often handles and the choices available to you.

What Read Often stores

Your books, shelves, reading sessions, goals, reminders, notes, and processed book artwork are stored on your device. Read Often does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Account information

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, Firebase Authentication processes an account identifier and may process the name and email address provided by that service. You can use Read Often as a guest and can permanently delete your Read Often account from Profile → Account.

Cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional. When enabled, supported library information is stored in Google Firebase under the unique account identifier created for the Apple or Google sign-in method you used. Apple and Google sign-ins remain separate accounts. Read Often does not require cloud sync to use the app.

Camera and photos

Access is requested only when you scan an ISBN or create cover, spine, or fore-edge artwork. Processing happens on your device. Camera originals are not saved by Read Often; only the cropped artwork you approve is copied into private app storage.

Book catalog services

When you search for a book or ISBN, the search text may be sent to NeoDB, Open Library, and Google Books to return metadata and cover images. These providers may receive standard network information such as your IP address.

Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. RevenueCat receives purchase, subscription, app, device, and entitlement information so Read Often can offer, verify, restore, analyze, and manage Plus access. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app identifier for guests and the Firebase user identifier when you are signed in. Read Often and RevenueCat do not receive your payment-card details.

Product analytics and advertising attribution

Read Often uses Firebase Analytics to understand screens, onboarding completion, feature usage, paywall performance, reading-session outcomes, and purchase outcomes. AppsFlyer measures app installs, attributed opens, and selected privacy-safe conversion events used to understand advertising performance. These services may process a pseudonymous app or device identifier, IP address, general app and device information, and interaction events. The advertising identifier is available only when you grant Apple’s tracking permission. Read Often never sends book titles, authors, ISBNs, notes, library names, search text, or onboarding answers to analytics or advertising partners.

Screen Time and notifications

Both are optional. App and category selections used for distraction blocking are handled through Apple’s Family Controls frameworks. Reminder details are used only to schedule notifications you request.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Read Often shares or exports library information only when you initiate that action or enable cloud sync. Local data remains until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Deleting a Read Often account removes its Firebase account and cloud library copy, but does not automatically erase the local library so you do not lose books unintentionally.

Children

Read Often is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
