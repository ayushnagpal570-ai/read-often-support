Delete your Read Often account.

You can delete your Read Often account at any time, from the app or by email. Reading as a guest never creates an account.

In the app

On iPhone, open Read Often and go to Profile → Account → Delete account…. On Android, go to the Settings tab → Account → Delete account. Confirm, then sign in again with the same Apple or Google account so we know it is you.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email ayushnagpal570@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Read Often account deletion”. Tell us whether you used Apple or Google sign-in. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your Read Often account in Firebase Authentication and the cloud copy of your library stored under it.

What stays on your device

Books, shelves, notes and reading history saved on your phone stay there, so you do not lose your library by accident. Remove them in the app or by uninstalling Read Often.

Purchases and analytics

Purchase records kept by Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat, and pseudonymous analytics and attribution events, are not linked to your name. Ask by email and we will request their deletion too. Records a store must keep for tax or legal reasons may be retained for as long as the law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
